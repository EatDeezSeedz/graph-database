                                                                  under eccentric conditions to decelerate knee extension
                                                                  and hip flexion.4,12 Similarly, the gastrocnemius muscle
                                                                  limits ankle dorsiflexion during running and other ath-
                                                                  letic activities. In both of these situations, it is likely that
                                                                  the muscles function to absorb the energy of the joint
                                                                  motion. It is unlikely that the muscles function eccentri-
                                                                  cally until a given length is reached or until a given force
                                                                  is attained. Instead, the muscles function eccentrically
                                                                  until the energy in the moving limb is absorbed and con-
                                                                  centric function can begin. It is therefore reasonable to
                                                                  expect that if fatigue decreases the ability of muscles to
                                                                  absorb energy, fatigue might be an important factor in the
                                                                  cause and prevention of muscle injury.
