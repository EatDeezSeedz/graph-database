Kanayama et al. (2015) tendon rupture risk in AAS users

This study shifts the AAS folder from lab and hypertrophy outcomes into real-world injury risk. Compared with similarly experienced non-using bodybuilders, long-term AAS users had a much higher lifetime incidence of tendon rupture, with the excess risk concentrated in upper-body tendons.

Design
Cross-sectional cohort
Sample
88 AAS users, 54 nonusers
Main risk

Main pattern
Upper-body ruptures drove the difference

Lifetime Tendon Rupture Incidence

At least one lifetime tendon rupture was reported by 22% of long-term AAS users versus 6% of comparison weightlifters.

AAS users Nonusers

Upper vs Lower Body Ruptures

The striking group difference came from upper-body tendon ruptures, which occurred only in the AAS users.

Total Rupture Burden

Several men had multiple separate lifetime ruptures. The AAS group accumulated 27 total ruptures versus 5 in the nonuser group.

Total ruptures

Context of Injury

Most tendon ruptures did not occur during weightlifting itself. Only 19% happened while lifting, and 26% were preceded by local pain.
