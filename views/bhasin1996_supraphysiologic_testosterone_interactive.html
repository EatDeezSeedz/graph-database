Bhasin et al. (1996) supraphysiologic testosterone and exercise

This NEJM study is the classic four-group testosterone-by-training experiment. Ten weeks of 600 mg/week testosterone increased fat-free mass, muscle size, and strength even without training, but the largest gains came when testosterone was combined with structured resistance exercise.

Design
2x2 factorial
Dose
600 mg/week
Duration
10 weeks
Main pattern
Testosterone plus training was highest
