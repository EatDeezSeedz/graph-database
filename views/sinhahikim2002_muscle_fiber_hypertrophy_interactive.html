AAS

Sinha-Hikim et al. (2002) muscle fiber hypertrophy

This mechanistic follow-up to the testosterone dose-response work shows where the size gains came from: higher testosterone exposure increased quadriceps and vastus lateralis volume, enlarged both type I and type II fibers, and increased myonuclear number, especially at 300 and 600 mg/week.
