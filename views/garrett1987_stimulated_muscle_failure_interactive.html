Garrett et al. (1987) stimulated vs passive muscle failure

This study compared passive rabbit EDL muscle with wave-summated and tetanically stimulated muscle when rapidly lengthened to failure. Stimulation increased the force required to tear the muscle-tendon unit and markedly increased the energy absorbed before failure, while the failure site still remained the distal musculotendinous junction.
