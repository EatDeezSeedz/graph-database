AAS

Piovesan et al. (2012) nandrolone and muscle repair

This paper follows cryoinjured rat tibialis anterior muscle across the repair timeline. Rather than a pure hypertrophy study, it suggests nandrolone decanoate shifted regeneration forward by increasing MyoD and myogenin expression at key stages and by reducing edema, myonecrosis, and inflammatory delay.

Model
Rat TA cryoinjury
Drug
Nandrolone decanoate
Dose
5 mg/kg twice weekly
Main takeaway
Nandrolone accelerated repair signaling

Study Notes

This is useful in the AAS folder as a repair/regeneration paper rather than a mass-only paper.

Early-phase signal
MyoD was significantly higher in the cryoinjured nandrolone group at day 7, which fits an accelerated activation and proliferation phase during repair.
Late-phase signal
Myogenin was significantly higher at day 21 in the cryoinjured nandrolone group, supporting later differentiation and maturation of regenerating fibers.
Morphology
The authors report cleaner histology with nandrolone: no edema or myonecrosis at day 7, and no edema or inflammatory infiltrate at day 14 in the cryoinjured treated group.
