Zhao et al. (2008) testosterone vs dexamethasone atrophy

This paper is best read as a mechanism study rather than a hypertrophy trial. In rats and androgen-receptor-expressing myotubes, testosterone blocked dexamethasone-driven atrophy signals, normalized MAFbx expression, and prevented the rise in protein degradation.

Catabolic stimulus

Study Notes

The paper’s most useful contribution for the AAS folder is mechanistic rather than size/performance based.

In vivo result
When testosterone was started at the same time as dexamethasone, it prevented the drop in gastrocnemius mass and reduced the rise in MAFbx mRNA.
Cell result
In androgen-receptor-expressing C2C12 myotubes, testosterone prevented dexamethasone-induced protein degradation and reduced MAFbx expression.
Transcriptional angle
The paper reports that testosterone blocked dexamethasone-induced activation of the human MAFbx promoter, while mRNA half-life was unchanged, pointing toward transcriptional regulation rather than altered transcript stability.
