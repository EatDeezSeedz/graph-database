Taylor et al. (1999) nandrolone in immobilized muscle

This rabbit study tested whether nandrolone decanoate could blunt disuse loss during casting. Even in sedentary animals, treated rabbits gained more body weight and showed greater muscle wet weight, dry weight, and contractile force in both casted and noncasted hindlimbs.

Model
Sedentary rabbits
Drug
Nandrolone decanoate
Dose
15 mg/kg weekly
Main takeaway
Anabolism persisted during immobilization

Body Weight Gain

Treated rabbits gained more total body weight than controls at 2, 4, and 8 weeks.

Extensor Digitorum Longus Force

Max contractile force was higher in the treated group, with the clearest separation by week 8 in both casted and noncasted limbs.

Tibialis Anterior Force

The tibialis anterior showed the same direction: treated rabbits produced more force in both immobilized and free limbs, especially by week 8.

Muscle Dry Weight

Dry weights were consistently higher in treated rabbits, supporting true tissue gain rather than just fluid retention.
