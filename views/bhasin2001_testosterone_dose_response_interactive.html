AAS

Bhasin et al. (2001) testosterone dose response

Weekly testosterone enanthate doses from 25 to 600 mg, given with GnRH agonist suppression, produced a clean dose-response across circulating testosterone, lean mass, thigh muscle size, leg performance, hemoglobin, and HDL. The strongest anabolic changes appeared at 300 and 600 mg/week.

Protocol
20 weeks
Dose range
25 to 600 mg/week
Population
Healthy young men
Main pattern
